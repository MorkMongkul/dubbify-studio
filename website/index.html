Give Chinese videos a Khmer voice.

A Mac app for dubbing Chinese dramas, short videos and ads into Khmer. It translates every line, gives each character a natural voice, and keeps the original music.

Download for Mac See how it works

For Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 11 or later.

From the original to the dub

  1. Import

    Drop in a video. The dialogue is separated from the music and sound effects.

  2. Analyze

    Every line is transcribed, matched to its character and translated into Khmer.

  3. Edit

    Rewrite lines the way your audience talks. The Assistant keeps names consistent across episodes.

  4. Voice and export

    Give each character a natural Khmer voice, then export an MP4 with Khmer subtitles and the original music.

Which one is my Mac?

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. A chip such as Apple M1, M2 or M4 means Apple Silicon; a processor made by Intel means Intel.

Installing it

Dubify Studio isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it the first time. After that it opens like any other app.

  1. Open the download and drag Dubify Studio into Applications.
  2. Open it. When macOS says it can’t verify the app, click Done.
  3. In System Settings, open Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and confirm with your password.
  4. In the app, open Settings and paste your Gemini API key from Google AI Studio.
macOS says the app is damaged?

That’s macOS’s download quarantine, not a broken file. Run this in Terminal, then open the app again.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Dubify Studio.app"

Questions

Anything else? Ask on GitHub.

Does it run on Windows?

Not yet. Dubify Studio is for Macs with macOS 11 or later.

Do my videos leave my Mac?

Your projects, videos and exports are saved on your Mac. To do the AI work, the app sends audio and text to cloud services: Google’s Gemini API, using your key, for transcription and translation, and services hosted on Hugging Face for separating the music and generating voices.

How do updates work?

Dubify Studio checks for updates when it opens and installs them with one click. You can also choose Check for Updates… in the Dubify Studio menu. Version 0.1.0 can’t update itself: download the latest version once and replace it. Your projects are kept.

Which languages does it support?

It’s built for Chinese to Khmer: Chinese speech in, Khmer subtitles and voices out.

Why does it take a moment to open?

Each time it opens, Dubify Studio starts its own processing engine in the background. That can take up to a minute; you’ll see a start-up screen until it’s ready.

Where are my projects stored?

In ~/Library/Application Support/studio.dubify.desktop. If something goes wrong, the logs in ~/Library/Logs/studio.dubify.desktop show what happened.
