Give Chinese videos a Khmer voice.

Dubify Studio is a Mac app for dubbing Chinese videos into Khmer. It separates the dialogue from the music, transcribes and translates every line, and gives each character a natural Khmer voice. You have the final say on every word.

Download for Mac How to install it

For Apple Silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 11 or later.

How a dub comes together

Five steps, in the order you take them. Dubify Studio handles the slow parts; the decisions about words and voices stay with you.

YouDubify Studio

  1. Import

    You

    Drop a video onto the window.

    Dubify Studio

    Separates the dialogue from the music and sound effects, so the original soundtrack carries into the dub.

  2. Analyze

    You

    Click Analyze.

    Dubify Studio

    Transcribes every line with its timing, tells the speakers apart, and translates each line into Khmer. In later episodes it matches voices to the characters you’ve already named.

  3. Edit

    You

    Rewrite lines so they sound the way your audience talks. Name the characters and fix anything misheard.

    Dubify Studio

    Keeps every line in sync with the picture. The Assistant can find a name or phrase across all your episodes and suggest consistent wording.

  4. Voice

    You

    Give each character a voice: design one from a description, or clone one from a short clip.

    Dubify Studio

    Generates every line in its character’s voice. Auto-fit voices then speeds each clip up or down to fill the time the original line took.

  5. Export

    You

    Add Khmer subtitles, your logo, and blur boxes over the old subtitles, then click Export.

    Dubify Studio

    Mixes the Khmer voices with the original music and effects, and saves an MP4 wherever you choose.

Lines that fit the scene

A first-pass translation is a draft. Khmer picks its words by who is speaking to whom, so a line that’s correct on paper can sound cold or stiff in a character’s mouth.

Every line sits beside its original, so you can fix the wording before any voice is generated.

00:14:31 Xiaoyu

你终于回来了。

First pass

ទីបំផុតអ្នកបានត្រឡប់មកវិញ។

Your edit

ទីបំផុតបងក៏ត្រឡប់មកវិញ។

She’s greeting her husband after a long time apart. អ្នក is a neutral “you”; a wife would say បង.

Which one is my Mac?

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it lists a chip such as Apple M1, M2 or M4, choose Apple Silicon. If it lists a processor made by Intel, choose Intel.

You’ll need

  • macOS 11 Big Sur or later.
  • An internet connection. Transcription, translation and voices run on cloud AI services.
  • A Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. Setting it up takes a couple of minutes.

Installing Dubify Studio

Dubify Studio isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, macOS asks you to confirm it. After that, it opens like any other app.

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Dubify Studio into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Dubify Studio from Applications. macOS says it can’t verify the app; click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, choose Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security. Next to “Dubify Studio” was blocked, click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Open Anyway again and enter your Mac’s password.

The Open Anyway button stays for about an hour after step 2. On macOS 14 or earlier, you can instead Control-click the app, choose Open, then click Open.

If macOS says the app is damaged

That message comes from macOS’s download quarantine, not from a broken file. Open Terminal, paste this command, press Return, then open the app again.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Dubify Studio.app"

Every download is built by GitHub Actions from the public source code.

Before your first dub

  1. Get a Gemini API key

    Sign in to Google AI Studio and create an API key. Dubify Studio uses it to transcribe, translate and answer you in the Assistant.

  2. Add it in Settings

    Click Settings in the app’s left rail, paste the key into Gemini API key, and click Save. The key is stored only on your Mac.

  3. Import a video

    Drop a video onto the window. Once the dialogue and music are separated, click Analyze.

Questions

Does it run on Windows?
Not yet. Dubify Studio is for Macs with macOS 11 or later.
Do my videos leave my Mac?
Your projects, videos and exports are saved on your Mac. To do the AI work, the app sends audio and text to cloud services: Google’s Gemini API, using your key, for transcription and translation, and services hosted on Hugging Face for separating the music and generating voices.
Which languages does it support?
It’s built for Chinese to Khmer: Chinese speech in, Khmer subtitles and voices out.
Why does it take a moment to open?
Each time it opens, Dubify Studio starts its own processing engine in the background. That can take up to a minute, and you’ll see a start-up screen until it’s ready.
How do I update?
Dubify Studio checks for updates when it opens and installs them with one click. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the Dubify Studio menu. Version 0.1.0 can’t update itself yet: download the new version from this page once and drag it into Applications, replacing the old one. Your projects are kept either way.
Where are my projects stored?
In ~/Library/Application Support/studio.dubify.desktop. If something goes wrong, the logs in ~/Library/Logs/studio.dubify.desktop show what happened.
